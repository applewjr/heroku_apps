How boards and answers are served

Firsts numbers its puzzles (No. N is 2026-11-25 + N days), and the game decides which one is today, so boards are kept by puzzle. Future puzzles open early in the game, so you can enter and approve days ahead: an approval for a future number is served from its day.

A starting board a player enters is logged once they've looked at its results for a few seconds. It's served once {{ serve_networks }} players enter the same tiles, greens and lengths for that puzzle; players on one network count as one. A board loaded from today's boards counts only once it's changed. Your approval comes first, then the players' boards, most players first, at most {{ todays_boards_max }}. Older puzzles are offered only once you approve them.

Answers: open a board, tap ✓ on each word Firsts takes (or type it), then Approve. Your answers are served as checked straight away. A word {{ confirm_networks }} players say worked, more than say it was refused, is served as players found; one as many say was refused leaves that clue's list. A date in red has players agreeing on a board other than yours: the game may have changed the puzzle.
