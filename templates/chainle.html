How to use ▼
1. Pick the round. Rounds 1 to 3 are two-word bridges, round 4 has three clues, and round 5 asks for the closest meaning. If a round looks different, switch its type.

2. Type the clue words. The hint appears as soon as every clue is in.

3. Take a hint. First you see the phrases the best word makes with each clue, with the word hidden, and its length. Show first letter, then Show next letter, uncovers it one letter at a time. Below, Possible answers lists the best words, each hidden until you tap Show letter or Reveal, or Show all answers to see them all.

4. Copy it into the game. Tap a revealed word to copy it, then paste it into Chainle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Chainle?

Chainle is a daily word game with five rounds and one guess per round. In a bridge round you see two or three clue words and type one word that links them all. In the last round you type the word closest in meaning to a single clue. Each round is worth up to 1,000 points, and a word that fits every clue well scores more than one that fits only some of them.

How does this solver help without spoiling the answer?

Type the clue words for a round. The solver gives a hint first: the phrases the best word makes with each clue, with the word hidden, such as SNOW____ and FOOT____, and how many letters it has. Tap Show first letter for a bigger hint, then Show next letter for each letter after that. Below the hint, the best words are listed with every letter hidden. Tap Show letter to uncover one letter at a time, Reveal to see the whole word, or Show all answers to see every word at once. Tap a revealed word to copy it, then paste it into the game.

How does it find the linking word?

It measures meaning: word vectors, learned from how words are used across billions of words of text and from a network of what words mean, put related words close together, and the solver looks for the word closest to every clue at once, like HOSPITAL for DOCTOR and NURSE. A word close to one clue but far from another ranks low, because the weakest clue counts most. For the closest-meaning round it ranks the words nearest in meaning and leaves out opposites.

How accurate is it?

It suggests, it does not know the answer. Chainle has its own list of accepted answers for each round and scores other words by how close they are, so the top pick is often, but not always, the one the game wants. A word close to every clue scores well, and one tied strongly to only one clue scores poorly. Look at the next few picks as well, because you only get one guess.

Does it work for older puzzles?

Yes. The solver only needs the clue words you type, so it works the same for today's rounds, for past puzzles and for practice rounds, wherever you play Chainle.
