How to use ▼
1. Get the board in.{% if boards_on %} If today's board is offered above the board, tap it and everything fills in.{% endif %} Otherwise tap the first tile and type the letters on the keyboard below it, column by column from the left, each column top to bottom. A tile with a picture gets the Picture key. Then tap Mark tiles and tap each green tile once, and type the clues and their lengths (a clue is optional, its length isn't).

2. Hints or Answers. Hints go one step at a time for each clue: the tile its answer starts on, then the first half of its path, then the word. Start with the clue marked Start here: the solver is surest of it. Answers list the likeliest words for every clue. Tap a word to see its path on the board.

3. Tell it what worked. Tap ✓ on a word Firsts took and the clue is done; tap ✗ on one it refused. Not listed? Type it under the clue. When Firsts takes tiles away, tap Mark tiles and tap them out here too: the fewer tiles left, the surer the last answers get. Your marks help other players with the same board.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Firsts?

Firsts is a daily crossword from Bleppo Games, played on a hexagon of 19 letter tiles. Each of the ten clues is answered by tracing its word through neighbouring tiles, starting on a green tile: the green tiles are the answers' first letters. Tiles are shared between answers, and a tile goes away once every answer that uses it is solved.

How does this solver help without spoiling the puzzle?

Load or type the board, then pick Hints or Answers. Hints go one step at a time for each clue: first the tile its answer starts on, then the first half of its path, then the word. Answers list the likeliest words for every clue, each traced on the board. The page remembers which one you like.

How sure is the solver?

Every word it suggests can be traced on the board from a green tile, and has the clue's length. Which one the clue means is its best guess, from dictionary definitions and word meanings, so plain definitions are its strong side and puns its weak one. Checked means the day's answer was entered by hand on this site, and players found means other players said the game took it.

Why does marking tiles gone help?

Firsts takes a tile away once every answer that uses it is solved, and a green tile turns plain once every answer starting on it is solved. So the tiles left are exactly what the unsolved answers use. Keep the board here the same as the game's and the solver uses that: near the end, the last answers have to use up the tiles that are left.

What are the picture tiles?

Some boards have a tile with a picture instead of a letter. An answer through it spells the picture's word: a sun tile gives the SUN in SUNDAY. Type what the picture shows and the solver tries that word, and words close to it; once one answer through it works, every clue uses that reading.

Do I have to type the board in?

Often not. Once today's board has been entered on this site, it's offered above the board: tap it and the tiles and clues fill in. Check it matches your game.

When does a new Firsts puzzle come out?

Once a day. The puzzles are numbered: No. 001 came out on 26 November 2026, and each day's number is one more. Older puzzles stay playable in the game, and this page lists the older ones it has answers for.

About this tool

This is an unofficial helper for Firsts, provided for assistance and entertainment purposes only. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bleppo Games. It never connects to the game: its word data comes from public sources, listed under Data sources and licences below.
