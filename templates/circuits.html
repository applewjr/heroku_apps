How to use ▼
1. Type the board. Pick the board size that matches your game, 5 by 3 or 4 by 4. Tap a square and type the word on that tile, then press Enter to go to the next square. For an empty box, tap Box (or type ?); for a square with nothing in it, tap Blank.{% if boards_on %} Once other players have entered today's board, you can skip this: tap it above the board and it fills in for you.{% endif %}

2. Check the arrows. The page draws an arrow from each box to every square next to it, and your game may have fewer: a square next to two boxes often links to just one. Tap an arrow your game doesn't show to remove it, or a dashed one to add it. After a solve, an arrow ringed in gold is one the solver can't make a compound across, so check that one first.

3. Help or Solve. Help shows each box's length and the compounds it makes, with the word hidden: Show a letter uncovers one letter at a time, Show word the whole word. Solve shows every answer at once. Each box says how sure the solver is: Sure, Likely or Guess.

4. Keep what you've got. When the game accepts a word, tap Keep on its box's card, or tap it among the box's other fits. If the card isn't showing that word, type it in first. The solver works the other boxes out again around it. Used a Bolt? Type the letter it showed into that box's card and tap it starts with. Tap a revealed word to copy it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Circuits?

Circuits is Puzzmo's daily word puzzle. The board is a small grid of word tiles and empty, numbered boxes, joined by arrows. Each box takes one word that makes a compound term with every tile or box its arrows touch, read in the arrow's direction, left to right or top to bottom: SNOW and BALL make SNOWBALL, FIRE and STATION make FIRE STATION.

How does this solver help without spoiling the puzzle?

Type the board in, then pick Help or Solve. Help shows each box's length and the compounds it makes, with the word hidden, and a box to start with. Tap Show a letter to uncover one letter at a time, or Show word for the whole word. Solve shows every box's answer at once, with how sure the solver is about each. The page remembers which one you like.

How do I enter the board?

Pick the board size that matches your game, 5 by 3 or 4 by 4. Tap a square and type the word on that tile, then press Enter to move to the next square. For an empty box, tap Box or type a question mark; for a square with nothing in it, tap Blank. The page draws an arrow from each box to every square next to it: tap any arrow your game doesn't show to remove it, or a dashed one to add it.

{% if boards_on %}

Do I have to type the board in?

Often not. Players enter their boards into this solver as they play, so once other players have entered today's board, it's offered above the empty board: tap it and its tiles and arrows fill in for you. Puzzmo and Yahoo Games each have their own Circuits board, so some days more than one is offered; tap the one that matches your game, and check it before you trust the answers.

{% endif %}

How does it find the words?

It knows over a million compound words and two-word phrases, from dictionaries and from how often words sit side by side in Wikipedia. It fills every box at once, so each word has to join the tiles and boxes on all of its arrows, and the boxes check each other.

How accurate is it?

Most boxes, not all. Every box gets a label: Sure, Likely or Guess. A Guess usually means a compound the solver doesn't know, so look at its other fits. Solve the boxes you're sure of first and tap Keep on each, and the solver works the rest out again around them. Boxes marked Checked come from the real game.

Does it work for older puzzles?

Yes. The solver only needs the board you type, so it works for today's puzzle, for past ones and for practice boards alike.
