How boards are served

A new 4x6 comes out at each player's own midnight (the game goes by the device's date), so boards are kept by the player's date, and two dates are live at once around the world. This page shows Pacific tomorrow (players ahead of you), today and the two days before.

A board a player enters is logged once they've looked at its solution for a few seconds. It's served once {{ serve_networks }} players enter the same 24 tiles that date; players on one network count as one. A board loaded from today's boards counts only once it's changed. A board with no solution is never logged: the game's always has one. Your approval comes first, then the players' boards, most players first, at most {{ todays_boards_max }}. A board first seen on an earlier date isn't served unless you approve it for that date.

Check a board by its par: 4x6 shows the fewest moves plus {{ par_over }}. Approve is optional, since players' boards serve on their own. One approval a date: a new one takes the old one's place. Undo takes your approval back on every date (the players' boards decide again). Remove deletes that date's entries of the board, approvals too.
