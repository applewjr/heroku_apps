About these lists

The word form's entries are final: players can never reverse them, and the word's votes start over. Type the word, choose a list, then press Submit.

Include the colon to search the Board column: a whole board, or t: for every board with center T. A search stays through Remove, Add and Undo until you press Clear search.

Popularity is how common the word is in real-world English: log10 uses per billion words, about 1 (very rare) to 7.6 ("the"). Below 2 (red) is what the Smush solver tags rare; "none" means it isn't in the frequency list at all.

How boards are served

The boards players typed into the solver, logged once all nine letters are in, on their own date (Smush rolls over at each player's midnight). A board is just its letters: the game shuffles the eight outer tiles for every player. Served is the board players whose date that is are offered{% if not board_live %} (once it goes live){% endif %}, with its hints.

Your approval is served straight away, whatever the players typed, and the latest one for a day wins. Without one, the puzzle most players entered that day is served once {{ serve_crowd }} of them have, counting one player per network (an IPv6 /48, or one IPv4 address). Never a puzzle first seen on an earlier day (an older board), unless you approve it. The starting spicy letter comes from your approval, or from players who set it before playing a word: the letter most of them chose, none on a tie. Remove deletes the players' entries of that puzzle on that day; Undo takes back an approval and leaves the players' boards alone.
