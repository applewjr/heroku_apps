About these lists

Your entries are final: players can never reverse them, and the phrase's votes start over. Remove takes a phrase out of the solver's answers, whether it came from the shipped list or from players. Punctuation is ignored: DON'T and DONT are the same phrase.

Searching a whole phrase also says whether the solver lists it: a shipped phrase shows its tags and popularity, and an added one scores 40 in every category. Include the colon to search the Board column, e.g. song: for every song board. A search stays through Keep out, Add and Undo until you press Clear search.

How boards are served

A board is its word lengths and the category. No letters are ever stored, and never the phrase. The day is the game's, for every player, from the server's clock: a new puzzle goes live at 23:30 in Vilnius, 1:30pm Pacific most of the year. So until 1:30pm your date here is yesterday's, and that's the puzzle that is live.

Your approval is served straight away, whatever the players entered, and there's one a day: approving another board that day replaces it. Without one, a board is served once {{ serve_crowd }} players, one per network, enter the same lengths that day; then whichever has the most players, a tie going to the first to get there. Its category is the one most of them chose. The first words of a longer board at least as many entered are never served (half-typed), nor a board first seen on an earlier day in the last {{ past_days }} (players catch up on older puzzles); approving one serves it anyway.

A player's board is logged when they use it (a letter, a miss, a reveal, a copy) or leave with hints on screen. One loaded from the offer counts only once its shape is changed. Approve takes a board players entered. Undo takes the day's approval back and leaves the players' boards alone. Remove deletes the players' entries of that board on that day.
