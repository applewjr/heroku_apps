How to use ▼
1. Enter the board. Tap the colors in your game's order: left to right, top shelf first. Each tap fills the next square. Wrong one? Tap the square, then the right color. Partway through a game? Enter the board as it is now: a short shelf ends with Next (or tap the next shelf), and the Stage and Extra fill the same way.{% if boards_on %} Once other players have entered today's board, you can skip this: tap it above the board and it fills in and solves. If it isn't your board, tap ↩ Not my board.{% endif %}

2. Check the par. The page says what par your game should show. If yours is different, a tile was entered wrong.

3. Help or Solution. Help shows nothing until you ask, one step at a time. Solution lists every move, and Next walks through them on the board.

4. Follow along. Made a move of your own? Tap it on the board here: the shelf it came from, then where it went. The page says whether you're on track, and works out the rest from there.
