How to use ▼
1. Type the nine words. One word to a box, in any order: the game shuffles them anyway. Tab or Enter moves to the next box.{% if boards_on %} Once other players have entered today's board, you can skip this: tap it above the boxes and the words fill in.{% endif %}

2. Help or Solve. Help keeps the answer hidden until you ask: Narrow it down shows the likeliest hubs, and each group can show its theme (when the solver knows it), one of its words, or the whole group. Solve shows the hub and all four groups. Each group says how sure the solver is: sure, likely or a guess.

3. Tell it what the game said. After you try a guess in 4x3, tap the same three words on the board here and pick what the game said: Right, One away or Not even close (or Not sure which, if you didn't catch it). If you called the hub and it was right, tap the hub alone. The solver keeps everything you mark and works out the rest again, and your marks help other players with the same board.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is 4x3?

    4x3 is Hank Green's daily word puzzle. Nine words sit on a three by three grid. One of them, the hub, belongs to all four categories, and each of the other eight belongs to one, so every category is the hub and two more words. Find the three words of a category to solve it.

    How does this solver help without spoiling the puzzle?

    Type the nine words, then pick Help or Solve. Help keeps the answer hidden until you ask: narrow down the hub, then for each group see its theme when the solver knows it, one of its words, or the whole group. Solve shows the hub and all four groups at once. The page remembers which one you like.

    How sure is the solver?

    Each group says how sure it is: sure, likely or a guess. Categories about what words mean are its strong side. Trivia and wordplay it often can't know, so treat a guess as a guess. Checked means the day's answer was entered by hand on this site, and confirmed by players means other players marked the group right after the game took it.

    What do I do after I try a guess in the game?

    Tell the solver what 4x3 said. Tap the three words on the board here, then Right, One away or Not even close, or use the buttons on a group you tried. If it didn't take them and you didn't catch which of the two it said, pick Not sure which. The solver keeps what you learned and works out the rest again. Not even close means the hub is not one of those three words.

    Do I have to type the words in?

    Often not. Players enter their boards into this solver as they play, so once other players have entered today's board, it's offered above the empty boxes: tap it and the words fill in. Check it matches your game.

    {% endif %}

    When does a new 4x3 puzzle come out?

    At midnight where you are. The game picks the puzzle by your device's date, so players in different time zones get each day's puzzle at their own midnight.

    Does it work for older puzzles?

    Yes. The solver only needs the nine words you type, so it works for today's puzzle, for past ones and for puzzles friends share.
