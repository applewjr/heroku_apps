About these lists

The word form's entries are final: players can never reverse them, and the word's votes start over. Type the word, choose a list, then press Submit.

Searches the word lists. Include a colon to search the reports' Board column: a board is each column's letters in alphabetical order, with a colon between columns (abs:eio:lnr...), so the order players typed a column's letters in doesn't matter. A search stays through Remove, Add and Undo until you press Clear search.

Popularity is how common the word is in real-world English: log10 uses per billion words, about 1 (very rare) to 7.6 ("the"). Below 2 (red) is what the Typeshift solver tags rare; "none" means it isn't in the frequency list at all.

How boards are served

The game day is Puzzmo's: the date in America/Chicago, for every player, so a new board starts at midnight Central (10pm Pacific). A board a player started before midnight and logged after it counts for the day they started it, if that was yesterday.

Boards players typed are logged once they use the solver on them. A board is its columns: each column's letters in any order, column by column. It's served once {{ serve_players }} players enter the same board that day. Players on one network count as one. A board loaded from today's boards counts only once it's changed. Served are the boards offered that day, for each player to pick theirs: your approvals first, then boards the players settled, most players first, at most {{ todays_boards_max }}. For the first {{ yesterday_hours }} hours of a day, yesterday's are offered after them, labelled as yesterday's.

Not served without your approval for that day: a past puzzle (a board {{ serve_players }}+ players entered on an earlier day, from an archive replay or a late finisher), and a board that is only the first columns of a bigger board at least as many players entered that day (half-typed).

Approve: enter your game's board with Enter your board below, or press Add on a board you entered yourself on /typeshift (Add copies the most recently updated entry of that board, its time under the button, and approves nothing if a newer one came in before you pressed it). Undo takes the approval back and leaves the players' boards alone. Remove deletes that day's entries of the board, approvals too. Click a board to open it in the board editor, to check it, fix a column and approve it.
