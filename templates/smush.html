How to use ▼
Type your nine Smush letters into the grid - the gold center letter goes in the middle. Every playable word appears instantly, ranked by points using the game's exact scoring. One caveat: Smush's official word list isn't public, so the solver uses its own dictionary and the game may refuse some suggestions.

As you play: tap a tile to make it the 🌶 spicy letter, and hit ✓ Played on a word to auto-spend its letters. The spicy letter moves after every play, so tap the new spicy tile after each word. To fix a tile's remaining uses or change a letter, tap ✏️ Fix a tile first. If Smush refuses a word, hit ✗ and confirm to hide it - rejected words don't spend any letters, and your report helps us fix the word list. If Smush accepts a word we don't list, type it into the box under the results. Words marked ⚠ are rare - the ones Smush is most likely to refuse. Tap the ⚠ for details.

Joining a game already in progress? Tap ➕ Add played word under the board, then type in each word you've already used and hit ✓ Played. The solver spends those letters for you, so everything it suggests fits the uses you have left. It takes any word your board can spell, including ones missing from our list, so there's no hunting through the results for it.

Going for the full smush? The 🥞 All 8 tab plans a set of words that spends every remaining letter use exactly, flattening the whole board for the Clean Plate bonus. When it can, the plan starts with the ★ pangram - playing the pangram first and cleaning the plate earns Smush's PERFECT bonus, doubling your final score. Play the plan top to bottom: after the pangram it leads with its riskiest words, so if Smush refuses one there are still plenty of letters left to re-plan around. The plan recalculates whenever you play or reject a word.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Smush?

Smush is Hank Green's free daily word game. You get a 3×3 grid of nine letters and make words for points. Every word must use the gold center letter, which never runs out. Every other letter can only be used five times total. Using the spicy letter or spending a letter's last use (smushing it) multiplies that word's points, and every board hides a pangram that uses all nine letters.

How does this Smush solver work?

Enter your nine letters into the grid with the gold center letter in the middle. The solver instantly ranks every playable word by points using the game's exact scoring. Tap a tile to set the 🌶 spicy letter - or use ✏️ Fix a tile to adjust remaining uses - and mark words as played to automatically track letter wear as your game progresses.

Do I have to type today's letters?

Often not. Players enter their boards into this solver as they play, so once other players have entered today's board, it's offered above the grid: tap Load it and the nine letters fill in, each with all its uses. Check they match your game - the tiles can be in any order - and tap your game's 🌶 spicy tile if it isn't set already. Playing an older board? Type its letters as usual.

Can I enter words I already played?

Yes. Tap ➕ Add played word under the board, type in each word you've used, and hit ✓ Played - the solver spends its letters and adds it to your played pile, so the rest of its suggestions fit the uses you actually have left. This is the fast way to pick the solver up mid-game when you're down to the last few letters. The box accepts any word your board can spell, whether or not it's one we suggested, so you don't need to find it in the ranked list first. One caveat: it scores the word with whatever 🌶 spicy letter is set right now, so words you're catching up on can score low - the letters they spend are still exact.

How is a word scored in Smush?

Each letter has a point value similar to Scrabble, and a word's base score is the sum of its letter values. Bonuses then add to a single multiplier: +1 for each use of the 🌶 spicy letter, +1 for each letter the word 🥞 smushes flat (spends its last use), and +2 for a ★ pangram, or +4 if the pangram is your very first word. The word's points are its base score times that multiplier - so a word that uses the spicy letter once and smushes two letters scores ×4.

What is the best Smush strategy?

Play the pangram first - the +4 first-word bonus makes it worth roughly five times its base score, usually the biggest single play of the game. After that, chase words that combine the spicy letter with smushing letters flat, since every bonus adds another +1 to the multiplier. Lean on the center letter as much as possible: it is free and its points still count.{% if ice_live %} Or go for ICE COLD instead (below): never touch the 🌶 and your whole final score is ×5.{% endif %}

How do I find today's Smush pangram?

Enter today's nine Smush letters into the solver with the gold center letter in the middle. Every pangram in the word list is highlighted gold and marked with a ★, usually right at the top of the points ranking. Play it as your very first word for the biggest bonus - the pangram-first multiplier, and the setup for Smush's PERFECT score doubler.

Can this solver smush all 8 letters flat?

Yes - the 🥞 All 8 mode plans a set of words whose letter costs together spend every remaining use of all eight outer letters, flattening the whole board for Smush's Clean Plate bonus. When possible the plan starts with the ★ pangram: playing the pangram first and cleaning the plate earns the PERFECT bonus, which doubles your final score. Play the plan top to bottom - after the pangram it leads with its riskiest words, so a refusal lands while there are still letters left to re-plan around. If Smush refuses a word, hitting ✗ recalculates a fresh plan from the words that remain. When no combination in the dictionary can flatten everything, it shows the best plan it found and which letters get stranded.

What is ICE COLD in Smush?

ICE COLD is a hidden Smush bonus: play at least five words without any of them ever using the 🌶 spicy letter, and your whole final score is multiplied by 5. The spicy letter moves to a random tile after every word, so it takes a plan. On today's board, this solver's 🧊 Ice Cold tab is that plan: the word to play for wherever the 🌶 is, your chance of a clean ICE COLD finish, and the words to save for the end. Skip the ★ pangram - it always uses the spicy letter, so ICE COLD can't be combined with PERFECT, but ×5 is worth more than ×2. If every word left would use the 🌶, the tab tells you to stop: end the game with Smush's stuck? link and keep the ×5.

Will Smush accept every word this solver suggests?

Not always. Smush's official word list isn't public, so this solver runs on its own large open dictionary. Most suggestions work, but the game will refuse some of them - especially obscure words, which the solver marks with ⚠ based on real-world word frequency. When a word is refused, hit the ✗ button on it: it's hidden from the results without spending any letters, just like a refused guess in the real game. Your ✗ also tells us, and we take every report into account when updating the list.

Why don't my points match the game exactly?

This solver uses an open-source word list that may not perfectly match Smush's official list, and each Smush puzzle also hides a secret "editor's choice" word worth a flat +15 that can't be predicted. If Smush refuses a word we suggest, hit the ✗ on it. If Smush accepts a word we don't list, type it into the box under the results. We take every report into account when updating the list.
