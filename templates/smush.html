How to use ▼
Type your nine Smush letters into the grid - the gold center letter goes in the middle. Every playable word appears instantly, ranked by points using the game's exact scoring. One caveat: Smush's official word list isn't public, so the solver uses its own dictionary and the game may refuse some suggestions.

As you play: tap a tile to make it the 🌶 spicy letter, and hit ✓ Played on a word to auto-spend its letters. The spicy letter moves after every play, so tap the new spicy tile after each word. To fix a tile's remaining uses or change a letter, tap ✏️ Fix a tile first. If Smush refuses a word, hit ✗ to hide it - rejected words don't spend any letters. Words marked ⚠ are rare - the ones Smush is most likely to refuse. Tap the ⚠ for details.

Going for the full smush? The 🥞 All 8 tab plans a set of words that spends every remaining letter use exactly, flattening the whole board for the Clean Plate bonus. When it can, the plan starts with the ★ pangram - playing the pangram first and cleaning the plate earns Smush's PERFECT bonus, doubling your final score. The words work in any order (pangram first!), and the plan recalculates whenever you play or reject a word.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Smush?

Smush is Hank Green's free daily word game. You get a 3×3 grid of nine letters and make words for points. Every word must use the gold center letter, which never runs out. Every other letter can only be used five times total. Using the spicy letter or spending a letter's last use (smushing it) multiplies that word's points, and every board hides a pangram that uses all nine letters.

How does this Smush solver work?

Enter your nine letters into the grid with the gold center letter in the middle. The solver instantly ranks every playable word by points using the game's exact scoring. Tap a tile to set the 🌶 spicy letter - or use ✏️ Fix a tile to adjust remaining uses - and mark words as played to automatically track letter wear as your game progresses.

How is a word scored in Smush?

Each letter has a point value similar to Scrabble, and a word's base score is the sum of its letter values. Bonuses then add to a single multiplier: +1 for each use of the 🌶 spicy letter, +1 for each letter the word 🥞 smushes flat (spends its last use), and +2 for a ★ pangram, or +4 if the pangram is your very first word. The word's points are its base score times that multiplier - so a word that uses the spicy letter once and smushes two letters scores ×4.

What is the best Smush strategy?

Play the pangram first - the +4 first-word bonus makes it worth roughly five times its base score, usually the biggest single play of the game. After that, chase words that combine the spicy letter with smushing letters flat, since every bonus adds another +1 to the multiplier. Lean on the center letter as much as possible: it is free and its points still count.

How do I find today's Smush pangram?

Enter today's nine Smush letters into the solver with the gold center letter in the middle. Every pangram in the word list is highlighted gold and marked with a ★, usually right at the top of the points ranking. Play it as your very first word for the biggest bonus - the pangram-first multiplier, and the setup for Smush's PERFECT score doubler.

Can this solver smush all 8 letters flat?

Yes - the 🥞 All 8 mode plans a set of words whose letter costs together spend every remaining use of all eight outer letters, flattening the whole board for Smush's Clean Plate bonus. When possible the plan starts with the ★ pangram: playing the pangram first and cleaning the plate earns the PERFECT bonus, which doubles your final score. The words can be played in any order, and if Smush refuses one of them, hitting ✗ recalculates a fresh plan from the words that remain. When no combination in the dictionary can flatten everything, it shows the best plan it found and which letters get stranded.

Will Smush accept every word this solver suggests?

Not always. Smush's official word list isn't public, so this solver runs on its own large open dictionary. Most suggestions work, but the game will refuse some of them - especially obscure words, which the solver marks with ⚠ based on real-world word frequency. When a word is refused, hit the ✗ button on it: it's hidden from the results without spending any letters, just like a refused guess in the real game.

Why don't my points match the game exactly?

This solver uses an open-source word list that may not perfectly match Smush's official list, and each Smush puzzle also hides a secret "editor's choice" word worth a flat +15 that can't be predicted. If you find a word the solver missed or one Smush wouldn't accept, use the feedback button to let us know.
