How to use ▼
1. Type the board. Type each column's letters, top to bottom, into its own box: the first column in box 1, the second in box 2, and so on. Tab, Enter or Space moves to the next box, which is shown dashed after the last column you typed.{% if boards_on %} Once other players have entered today's board, you can skip this: tap it above the boxes and it fills in for you.{% endif %}

2. Help or Solve. Help shows how many words use each letter, rings in gold the letters only one word uses, and gives a hint for the hardest letter left: Show a letter uncovers its word one letter at a time, Show word the whole word. Tap a letter in the Letters left list for its own hint. Solve shows the fewest words that use every letter left, and every word that fits.

3. Keep up with your game. When the game turns letters solid, tap the same letters on the board here (tap again to undo), or tap ✓ on a word you played. The solver then plans only for the letters you have left. If Typeshift refuses a word, tap ✗ next to it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Typeshift?

Typeshift is Puzzmo's daily word puzzle. The board is a row of columns of letters. Slide the columns up and down to make a word across the middle row, one letter from each column. Each word you find turns its letters solid, and the goal is to turn every letter solid.

How does this solver help without spoiling the puzzle?

Type the board in, then pick Help or Solve. Help shows how many words use each letter and gives a hint for the hardest letter you have left: a word with that letter, hidden, that you uncover one letter at a time. Solve shows the fewest words that use every letter, and every word that fits. The page remembers which one you like.

How do I enter the board?

Type each column's letters from top to bottom into its own box. Tab, Enter or Space goes to the next box, which is always shown dashed after the last column you typed. When the game turns letters solid, tap the same letters on the board here, and the help covers only the letters you have left.

{% if boards_on %}

Do I have to type the board in?

Often not. Players enter their boards into this solver as they play, so once other players have entered today's board, it's offered above the empty board: tap it and the columns fill in for you. Check it matches your game before you trust the answers.

{% endif %}

When does a new Typeshift puzzle come out?

At midnight US Central time, for every player wherever they are: 10pm Pacific, 11pm Mountain and 1am Eastern. Puzzmo's day follows the date in Chicago.

Why is a word missing, or listed when the game won't take it?

The solver uses its own English word list, not Puzzmo's, so a few words differ. If Typeshift refuses a word, tap the cross next to it and choose that Typeshift refused it: the word is hidden for you, and your report helps fix the list. If the game takes a word the list lacks, send it with the box under the results.

Does it work for older puzzles?

Yes. The solver only needs the board you type, so it works for today's puzzle, for past ones and for practice boards alike.

About this tool

This is an unofficial helper for Typeshift, provided for assistance and entertainment purposes only. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Puzzmo. It never connects to Puzzmo: its words come from the public-domain ENABLE word list, ranked by how common each word is in English.
