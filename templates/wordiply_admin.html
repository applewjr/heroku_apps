How starters are served

The game day is Wordiply's: the date in Europe/London, for every player, so a new starter comes at London midnight (4pm Pacific most of the year). It's the server's clock, not the player's.

A starter is logged once a player uses the solver on it: they tap a word, Define or Show more, or leave the page for their game with its words on screen. A starter loaded from the offer counts only once it's changed. It's served once {{ serve_crowd }} players enter the same one that day; then whichever has the most players, a tie going to the first to reach {{ serve_crowd }}. Players on one network count as one. A starter that a longer one at least as many players entered begins with is never served on its own (half-typed).

Your approval is served straight away, whatever the players typed, and there's one a day: approving another starter that day replaces it. Approvals are optional; the players' starters serve without them. Add approves a starter players entered. Undo takes the day's approval back and leaves the players' starters alone. Remove deletes the players' entries of that starter on that day.
