Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Blossom word game?

Blossom is Merriam-Webster's free daily word puzzle. You are given a center letter and six surrounding petal letters and must find words of 4 or more letters - each word must contain the center letter. Clicking a petal doubles your score for any word containing that bonus letter.

Is this Blossom solver free to use?

Yes, completely free. No account, no sign-up, and no download required. Just enter your 7 letters and instantly see all valid words ranked by score.

How do I use the Blossom word finder?

Type your center letter into the yellow circle, then enter your six petal letters into the surrounding petals. Once all 7 letters are filled in, click any petal to see all valid words that score double with that bonus letter, ranked from highest to lowest score. Check off words as you play them to track your progress.

Do I have to type today's letters?

Often not. Players enter their letters into this solver as they play, so once other players have entered today's board, it's offered above the flower: tap Load it and all seven letters fill in, each in its spot. Check they match your game, then tap a petal. Playing an older puzzle from the archive? Type its letters as usual.

Why are some words missing from the results?

This solver uses an open-source word list that may not perfectly match Merriam-Webster's official game word list, so players help correct it. If Blossom rejects a word we list, tick its Invalid box. If Blossom accepts a word we don't list, type it into the box under the results. We take every report into account when updating the list. You can also use the "Report Invalid/Missing Word" button.

About this tool

This is an unofficial solver for Merriam-Webster's Blossom word game, provided for assistance and entertainment purposes only. The word list comes from an open-source GitHub repository and may not always align with the official game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Merriam-Webster.
