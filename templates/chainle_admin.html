About these lists

Remove takes a word off the solver's suggestions. Allow keeps a word on them that the solver would leave out as a plural or other -s form (a real word that only looks like one), and player reports can't take it off. Your entries are final: players can never reverse them, and the word's votes start over. Type the word, choose, then press Submit.

Searches the word lists. Include a colon to search the reports' Round column: a round is its clue words in alphabetical order with a colon between them. A search stays through Remove, Allow and Undo until you press Clear search.

Popularity is how common the word is in real-world English: log10 uses per billion words, about 1 (very rare) to 7.6 ("the"). "none" means it isn't in the frequency list at all.

How rounds are offered

A player's round is logged on their own date when they use it (a hint, a reveal, a copy, a report) or leave it solved. A round they loaded and left as it is never is, and nothing you do on /chainle_dev is. Players never say which site they play on: under an empty round they see the clues each of the day's sets has for it, tap the one in their game, and every empty round of that set fills in.

A round counts once {{ serve_crowd }} players have typed the same clue words, one per network (an IPv6 /48, or one IPv4 address). Two rounds belong to one day's set once {{ link_min }} players typed both. Within a set, a round needs more than half of that round's players; a tie leaves it blank. A players' set is offered only once all five of its rounds are settled; one with fewer isn't shown here. Which round it is: three words make round 4 and one word round 5; a two-word round goes in the latest of tabs 1-3 that at least two of its players used (players who come in mid-game type a later round into tab 1).

Your approvals come first, with their site's name on the button, and the players' set that shares a round with one fills its blank rounds. At most {{ todays_sets_max }} sets a day: your approvals, then the players' sets, most players first.

An approval is a whole set: approving a site's set replaces whatever you approved for it that day, and a round you leave blank is just not in it. You can approve your own typing, a set the players settled, or a set players typed, even one player. Sets players typed lists only players who typed all five rounds. Undo takes the site's set back. Remove on a set the players settled deletes every player entry of its rounds that day; on a set players typed, only those players' entries. Use it for junk, or staging tests (staging writes the prod table).
